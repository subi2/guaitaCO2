What it is

A pedagogical environmental decision tool

guaitaCO2 lets you choose materials and solutions by environmental criteria, not only by cost. You work with a project (elements with quantities), assign ÖKOBAUDAT datasets (EN 15804+A2) and see the CO₂e per module, the hotspots and the effect of substituting materials. It is meant for students and professionals.

Everything runs locally: the ÖKOBAUDAT CSV, projects and results are processed in the browser and stored locally (IndexedDB). Nothing is sent to any server. The app includes no ÖKOBAUDAT data. Note: the app interface is currently in Catalan only.

Getting started

From the CSV to the first result
  1. Download “ÖKOBAUDAT 2024-II as a CSV file with a period as the decimal separator” from oekobaudat.de (or use the app's Descarrega ÖKOBAUDAT button, which opens the direct link).
  2. In the app, Fitxer ▸ Importa ÖKOBAUDAT (CSV)… or drag the file onto the window. It is read locally (~50 MB, a few seconds) and stored in the browser.
  3. In Biblioteca, search for a material and press + to add it to the Obra (project).
  4. Enter the quantity and unit; check the total and change the counted modules in the top bar.
  5. With the ⇄ icon of an element, look at alternatives and substitute.

Modules and indicators

EN 15978 / EN 15804+A2
ModuleMeaning
A1–A3Product (raw materials, transport to factory, manufacturing)
A4–A5Transport to site and installation
B1–B7Use: maintenance, repair, replacement, refurbishment, operational energy and water
C1–C4End of life
DBenefits and loads beyond the system boundary; shown separately and not added

Indicators in kg CO₂e: GWP-total (= fossil + biogenic + luluc), fossil, biogenic and luluc. They are chosen in the top bar, as is the output in kg or t.

A “·” cell means module not declared, not zero. Elements with partial data are flagged (PAR/SENSE status) and the total is a minimum.

Library

Exploring ÖKOBAUDAT

Category tree on the left; filters for text search, unit, country, type (average, generic, specific, template, representative) and module with data. The table shows modules as colour-grouped columns with a heat map; the Columnes buttons hide or show groups. Click the name or double-click: full sheet (scenario variants, physical data, link to the original dataset and an optional own price). Columns can be sorted and exported to CSV.

Project

Elements, quantities and conversions

Each element has a name, group, dataset, quantity, unit, conversion factor and optional price. If the element's unit differs from the dataset's (e.g. m³ vs kg), the app proposes a factor from the dataset's density, area weight or linear weight; you can edit it. Elements can be added by hand, from the library, from a CSV or from a guaitaPreus selection. The footer shows totals per module and cost. The element dialog lets you choose the scenario variant of each module.

Layered solutions

Edita ▸ Solucions per capes…: define a wall, slab or roof as a list of layers (dataset + quantity per m² + factor + service life). They are saved in the project (they travel with the JSON) and/or in the browser's local library, and behave as a single element in the project. A module is “complete” only if all layers declare it. You can also turn an element into a solution from its dialog.

Service life and B4

Each element (or layer) can have a service life in years. With the study period (50 years by default), B4 = (⌈period/life⌉−1) × (declared A1–A5 + C1–C4). In the top bar you choose whether the dataset B4 or the service-life B4 is counted; the project always shows both values.

Multiple selection

Tick the boxes (with Shift for ranges) to change group, service life or price, duplicate or delete several elements at once. In the library, filters are remembered and the table is virtualised.

Alternatives

Substituting by environmental criteria

Shows datasets with the same unit and category with Δ CO₂e, Δ % and, if prices exist, Δ €, the quadrant (less CO₂e and less cost, etc.) and the extra cost per tonne avoided, on a Δ€ vs ΔCO₂e decision plane. You can substitute in the active scenario or create a new one. The category level is chosen automatically (the most specific with at least 3 alternatives) and can be changed. For layered solutions, the alternatives are other solutions in the library, or you change a material with ⇄ on the layer.

Compare at equal functional unit and check that the alternative fulfils the same function (strength, insulation, durability). CO₂e does not guarantee it.

Scenarios and analysis

Comparing and finding hotspots

Scenarios: copies of the project with substitutions; totals, phases and element-by-element differences are compared. Analysis: summary cards, phases (A, A4–A5, B, C), element groups, a Pareto of the heaviest elements and, optionally, kg CO₂e/m². The waterfall explains, element by element, the difference between two scenarios. In Analysis, if you enter the floor area, the result is compared with per-m² references (LETI 2020/2030, RIBA 2030, with cited source) and with your own; they are only comparable if you count the same modules. The shadow carbon price (€/t) is empty and inactive by default; if you set it, “cost + CO₂e × price” is shown.

Teaching cases

For teaching

Fitxer ▸ Exporta ▸ Cas didàctic creates a JSON with the brief, a goal (minimum CO₂e reduction and maximum cost increase) and the project structure. When opened, the goal bar (student mode) appears: it shows live whether the CO₂e reduction and the cost limit are met relative to the first scenario. The brief is read in Ajuda ▸ Enunciat del cas, and “Surt del cas” removes the bar. Cases include no database of their own, only the values of the datasets they use.

Limitations and source

Keep in mind
  • ÖKOBAUDAT is a German database: useful for orders of magnitude and relative decisions, not for certifying or replacing a building LCA or an EPD.
  • ÖKOBAUDAT is not designed for the LCA of a single product. Do not mix A1–A3 data from one database with modules from another.
  • The carbon price is a decision criterion, not a real cost.
  • Data stays in the browser: save the project as JSON to keep it.

Data: ÖKOBAUDAT, BMWSB / BBSR (oekobaudat.de), free to use citing the source. The app does not redistribute the data.

FAQ

Why “·” instead of 0?

Because the module is not declared in the dataset. A 0 means it is declared and equals zero.

Where is my data stored?

In the browser (IndexedDB). If you clear the site data you will lose it: save the project as JSON.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It is a single self-contained HTML file; you only need the downloaded ÖKOBAUDAT CSV.
